Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Draft notice: This policy has not yet been reviewed by MaBap Foundation's legal advisor. It is published here to be transparent about what this website currently does with the information you provide, and will be finalised before the Donate form is connected to a live payment processor.

What Information We Collect

The Donate form on this website asks for your full name, email address, PAN (Permanent Account Number), donation amount and whether your gift is one-time or monthly. We ask for your PAN and email specifically because Indian tax law requires them to issue an 80G tax-exemption receipt in your name.

What Happens to It Right Now

This website is currently a static site with no backend server or database. When you submit the Donate form today, the information you enter is processed only inside your own browser to show a confirmation message — it is not transmitted, stored, or seen by anyone at MaBap Foundation through this form as it stands. The form is not yet connected to a live payment gateway.

This means: no live donations can currently be completed through this page, and no personal data submitted here is currently retained anywhere.

What Will Change Once Payments Go Live

Once MaBap Foundation connects this form to a real payment processor, the information above will be sent securely to that processor solely to complete your donation and generate your 80G receipt. We will name that processor here and link to their own privacy policy at that time. We do not sell, rent or otherwise share your personal information for marketing purposes.

Cookies & Analytics

This website does not currently use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics software. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Data Retention

Once live donations begin, we will retain the minimum personal information necessary to issue tax receipts and meet statutory record-keeping requirements for a registered Section 8 non-profit, and no longer than required by law.

Your Rights

You may write to us at mabapfoundation@gmail.com to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to request that it be deleted, subject to any records we are legally required to keep (such as donation receipts for tax purposes).

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to mabapfoundation@gmail.com.
