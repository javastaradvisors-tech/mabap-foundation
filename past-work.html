Our Past Work

Our Journey, Year by Year

From a single online discourse in 2020 to 26 Toy & Games Libraries reaching 9,500+ children today — click into any year below to see what we've built together.

MaBap Foundation took its first steps in 2020 — formally registered as a Section 8 non-profit company, and almost immediately putting its mission into practice with its very first public programme.

  • 16 Sep

    MaBap Foundation is formally incorporated as a Section 8 non-profit company, laying the legal and spiritual foundation for everything that would follow.

  • 25 Oct

    Our very first public programme: an awareness lecture on Sanatana Dharma by Dr. Harish Joshi, "The Essence of Modern-Day Living," exploring how timeless spiritual wisdom applies to everyday life (Online, 35 attendees).

With the world still navigating the pandemic, 2021 saw the Foundation focus on immediate, tangible relief — health camps, food security and guidance through COVID-19 — while keeping its spiritual practice alive online.

  • 20–21 Mar

    Acupuncture Treatment Camp at Kalapurnam Tirth Dham, offering alternative-medicine treatment to those without easy access to conventional healthcare (50 beneficiaries).

  • 11 Apr

    An online guidance session on COVID-19 prevention and precaution, helping families navigate the second wave safely (100 beneficiaries).

  • 20 Jun

    International Yoga Day Celebration, combining yoga practice with guidance on recovering from post-COVID-19 complications, held online (50 beneficiaries).

  • 8 Aug

    Food Grain Distribution in Govandi, Mumbai, supporting families facing food insecurity during the pandemic (25 families).

  • 16 Aug

    Healthy Food Feeding and Education to Needy Little Angels, Vadodara — a recurring weekly programme combining nutritious food with values-based education for poor children (15 families).

  • 26 Sep

    Eye Camp of Cataract Operations at Vansada, restoring sight for people who could not otherwise afford treatment (10 persons).

  • 19 Nov & 25 Dec

    Two Acupuncture Treatment Camps at Dhanani Nagar Satsang Bhavan, Boisar, run jointly with Medicina Alternativa of Bharat (200 persons).

2022 marked the Foundation's first steps into direct, on-the-ground work with tribal communities — moving from online gatherings to physical outreach in the villages it would soon call home.

  • 26 Jan

    "Rashtra Devo Bhav!" — a Republic Day celebration held online, with Guruji and Guruma addressing participants on service to the nation (50 attendees). Full lecture video available — coming soon to Media

  • 13 Sep

    Shoes and t-shirts distributed to tribal children at Katil Pada School and the surrounding areas of Vevji, Talasari — the Foundation's first direct outreach in the region that would soon become home to its flagship Toy & Games Library programme (250 children).

This was the year everything changed. MaBap Foundation launched its flagship Toy & Games Library programme, transforming eleven schools in a single day and setting the pattern of growth that continues today.

  • 28 Jan

    Toy & Games Libraries launched in 11 schools across Vevji Village, Talasari in a single day — the founding moment of our flagship programme, giving tribal children regular access to toys and games that build motor skills, teamwork and imagination (~1,900 children reached).

  • 21 Jun

    International Yoga Day Celebrations at Andheri East, Mumbai, led by Yoga Teacher Mr. Pradeep Thakkar (50 beneficiaries).

  • 24 Oct

    The 12th Toy & Games Library launched at M.M. High School, Umbargaon, run by Umbargaon Education Society — extending the programme's reach well beyond its founding village (~2,100 students/year).

2024 was a year of scale. A landmark CSR partnership brought digital learning to hundreds of students, three more toy libraries opened their doors, and the Foundation extended its support beyond education into disaster relief.

  • 30 Mar

    15 Digital Classrooms installed across schools in Talasari, Maharashtra — a CSR project in partnership with Raj Borax Pvt. Ltd., bringing smart-screen, multimedia lessons to tribal and less-privileged students who had never had access to this kind of learning before (1,500+ students/year).

  • 27 Apr

    13th Toy & Games Library launched at Padmavati Thakordas Sanjanwala Balmandir, Umbergaon (50 students/year).

  • 27 Apr

    14th Toy & Games Library launched at Prathmik Shala Bhathi-Karambeli, Umbergaon (900 students/year). Donors: Shri Deepak S. Mehta & Smt. Bharati D. Mehta

  • 21 Jun

    International Yoga Day Celebrations at Andheri East, Mumbai (40 beneficiaries).

  • 29 Jun

    15th Toy & Games Library launched at Palgam Mukhyashala, Umargam (300 students/year). Donors: CA Ganesh Mehta & CA Deepak Kanabar

  • 6 Aug

    Tree Plantation drive on Babaji's Land at Umargaon, contributing to the greening of the land that will one day be home to Shri Siddha Guru Pith (30 participants).

  • Dec

    Funded the construction of a home for a nomadic family at Nichi Mandal, Tal. Morbi, Gujarat — extending the Foundation's seva beyond education into direct family support (₹75,000). Courtesy: Vicharata Samuday Samarthan Manch (VSSM)

  • 20 Dec

    16th Toy & Games Library launched at Solsumba Central Shala, Umargam (1,000 students/year). Donor: Kusum Jayantilal Charitable Trust

Toy & Games Libraries kept multiplying in 2025 — five more opened across Gujarat and Maharashtra, and the programme crossed a symbolic milestone, reaching its first school outside those two states, all the way in Bihar.

  • 19 Jan

    17th Toy & Games Library launched at Shri Swaminarayan Santan Mandir, Umargam (200 children/month).

  • 19 Feb

    18th Toy & Games Library launched at PM Shri Kumarshala, Umargam (100 students/year). Donor: Kusum Jayantilal Charitable Trust

  • 19 Feb

    19th Toy & Games Library launched at Adivasi Ashram Shala, Manekpur, Sanjan (150 students/year). Donor: Kusum Jayantilal Charitable Trust

  • 19 Feb

    20th Toy & Games Library launched at Ramkrishna Pimputkar Vidyalaya, Vevji Nanapada, Talasari (100 students/year). Donor: Kusum Jayantilal Charitable Trust

  • 10 Apr

    21st Toy & Games Library launched at MILT School, Simultala, Bihar — the first library outside Maharashtra and Gujarat (100 students/year). Donors: Dr. CA Mayur B. Nayak & Mr. Hitesh Mehta

By 2026, MaBap Foundation's Toy & Games Library network had grown to 26 libraries strong, reaching over 9,500 children a year — including a Silver Jubilee 25th library that made local news, and the programme's first entry into urban Mumbai. The year also saw the Foundation respond to a humanitarian crisis, delivering flood relief to devastated villages near the Varoli River in Umargam, and distributing new school uniforms to students at Bhatpada Primary School.

  • 2 Jan

    22nd Toy & Games Library launched at Smt. K.D. Shah and Shri D.V. Shah English Medium School, Madhi, Gujarat — the largest single-school beneficiary count in the programme's history (2,400 students/year). Donor: Kusum Jayantilal Baldev Charitable Trust

  • 5 May

    23rd Toy & Games Library launched at Damu Nagar, Kandivali East — the programme's first urban Mumbai library, set up at Rangoonwala Foundation (India)'s Community Centre (75 students/year). Sponsor: Shri Nitesh G. Mehta

  • 2026

    24th Toy & Games Library launched at Nadiol Prathmik Shala, Vadnagar, Gujarat (333 students/year).

  • 4 Jul

    A special milestone: the Silver Jubilee 25th Toy & Games Library launched at Bhalesara Prathmik Shala, Vadnagar, Gujarat — celebrated with a smiley-ball distribution for students and covered in local press, Sandesh News Kheralu and Aaj Janmabhoomi (209 students/year).

  • 2026

    26th Toy & Games Library launched at Sultanpura Prathmik Shala, Vadnagar, Gujarat (654 students/year).

  • 21 Jun

    International Yoga Day Celebrations at Shree Ghoghari Lohana Mahajan Bhavan, Andheri West, Mumbai (35 beneficiaries).

  • 26 Jul

    Flood Relief in Umargam — after unprecedented floods struck the Varoli River area on the night of 22–23 July (nearly 40 inches of rain in 36 hours, the worst in almost a century), Trustees Dr. CA Mayur B. Nayak and Mr. Hitesh Mehta visited the worst-affected villages of Kanchpada, Bhathi and Humaran. With support from Shri Suraj and his team at Swaminarayan Temple, MaBap Foundation distributed 150 ration kits, 200 shirts, 625 shorts and other clothing to families who had lost everything overnight. In partnership with Swaminarayan Temple, Umargam

  • Jul

    Milestone: 26 Toy & Games Libraries now running across Maharashtra, Gujarat and Bihar, benefiting more than 9,500 tribal and underprivileged students every year.

  • 14 Aug

    School Uniform Distribution at Bhatpada Primary School, Umargam — new uniforms handed out to students in partnership with Zee Uniforms, helping tribal children attend school with dignity and confidence.
