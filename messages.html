10 August 2026

Divine One, you are blessed. Babaji, thank You for blessing and supplying all of our needs — finances and faith, health and wealth, peace and love. Our flesh and our heart may fail, but You are the strength of our heart and our portion forever.

9 August 2026

Babaji, guide our hands to seva and our hearts to devotion. Let every act of service be an offering, and every offering a step closer to You.

8 August 2026

In stillness we find You; in service we express You. Let today's efforts be a quiet prayer of gratitude.

7 August 2026

You are the light that does not dim and the strength that does not tire. Walk with us, Babaji, in all that we undertake today.

6 August 2026

Let our minds be calm as the mountains and our hearts be open as the sky. Avirat seva mahe — devoted, always, to the seva of humanity.

5 August 2026

Every child we reach, every hand we hold, is Your grace working through us. Thank You, Babaji, for the privilege of service.
